# Methodology

This module models attributes of a place, not a judgement about which people
belong there. Research enters as a revisioned dossier, becomes concise sourced
signals, and may become numeric features only when evidence supports it.

Numeric features have three distinct meanings:

- `capacity`: more available provision is generally more of the thing.
- `intensity`: a magnitude a person may seek or avoid.
- `position`: a spectrum where a middle position can be preferable.

Those meanings must be preserved during matching. Structural derivation fills
only features with declared deterministic formulas; it is calibrated against
editorial values rather than treated as a hidden replacement for research.
Similarity and person matching compose resolved vectors at query time, with no
stored embedding or opaque prediction layer.
