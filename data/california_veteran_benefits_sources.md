# California veteran-benefits summary

Retrieved 2026-08-06. This statewide summary is used for every California city page.

- **Military retirement and Survivor Benefit Plan exclusion:** California Franchise Tax Board, [2025 Schedule CA (540) instructions](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-ca-instructions.html), lines 280-281. Qualifying 2025-2029 retirement/SBP income may be excluded up to $20,000 when federal AGI is no more than $125,000, or $250,000 for a surviving spouse or joint return. The $20,000 cap is per return, not per spouse.
- **Disabled Veterans' Exemption:** California State Board of Equalization, [Disabled Veterans' Exemption](https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/dv_exemption.htm). It reduces property tax liability through an assessed-value exemption on a qualifying principal residence; it is not a blanket property-tax waiver. The program covers qualifying 100%-rated service-connected disability and compensation at the 100% rate due to individual unemployability, among other statutory requirements.
- **2026 assessed-value amounts:** BOE [LTA 2025/014](https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta25014.pdf): $180,671 basic exemption; $271,009 low-income exemption; $81,131 low-income household limit for the January 1, 2026 lien date.
- **College Fee Waiver:** [CalVet College Fee Waiver](https://www.calvet.ca.gov/VetServices/Pages/College-Fee-Waiver.aspx). Eligibility and covered charges are plan-specific.
- **Disabled Veteran License Plates:** [California DMV](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-registration/license-plates-decals-and-placards/disabled-veteran-dv-license-plates/). Qualifying disabled veterans may receive registration and license-fee exemption for one eligible vehicle with DV plates.
- **Home lending:** [CalVet Home Loans](https://www.calvet.ca.gov/HomeLoans/Pages/New-Customer.aspx).

Benefit programs and annual thresholds change. The page links to the primary state sources and tells users to confirm individual eligibility.
