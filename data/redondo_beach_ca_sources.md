## 4. VA & Healthcare Access
- **Nearby VA Access**: Yes.
- **Nearest Outpatient VA Facility**: Gardena VA Clinic (1040 W Redondo Beach Blvd, Gardena, CA 90247), ~5 miles.
- **Nearest VA Medical Center**: VA Long Beach Healthcare System (5901 E 7th St, Long Beach, CA 90822), ~17 miles.
  - *Source*: U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs Facility Locator (https://www.va.gov/find-locations).
- **Veterans Tax & Benefits**: California partially excludes qualifying military retirement pay (up to $20,000 under AB 46/SB 312 for 2025–2029). 100% disabled veteran principal residence property-tax exemption. CalVet home loans and college fee waivers for dependents.
  - *Source*: CalVet (California Department of Veterans Affairs).

## 5. Defense & Tech Employer Hubs
- **Defense Hub**: Yes (`Y`). Northrop Grumman Space Systems headquarters and Space Park campus is located directly inside Redondo Beach, CA (1 Space Park Dr, Redondo Beach, CA 90278), employing thousands of aerospace engineers and scientists working on defense satellites, space telescopes, and lasers.
- **Tech Hub**: Yes (`Y`). Part of the South Bay / Silicon Beach high-tech aerospace corridor.
- **Retail Access**: `HasWalmart: N`, `HasCostco: N` (Costco and Walmart stores exist in neighboring Torrance).
