# Greenville, TX Sources

- **Population**: 2020 U.S. Census (28,164)
- **Housing**: Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI) for Greenville, TX as of mid-2026 ($243,421).
- **Taxes**: Tax Foundation / Avalara (6.25% state + 1.5% city + 0.5% county = 8.25%).
- **Politics**: Hunt County 2016 and 2024 Presidential Election results (ClarityElections / State sources). Trump won ~78.0% of the two-party vote in 2024, compared to ~78.9% in 2016.
- **Safety**: FBI UCR / NIBRS historical data (violent crime ~269 per 100k, index ~71 compared to US average). Rated as "Low".
- **LGBTQ**: No local protections found. Rated as "Not Rated" / HRC MEI.
- **Climate**: NOAA U.S. Climate Normals for Greenville/Majors Airport station.
- **VA Access**: Nearest VA is Greenville VA Clinic.
- **Defense Hub**: L3Harris has a major ISR Systems facility in Greenville.
- **Amenities**: Has Walmart Supercenter, no Costco in city limits (nearest is Rockwall).
