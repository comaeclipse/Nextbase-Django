## Guardrail evidence

- The 2020 CDE response is a year-end annual dump: January-November keys are zero, December carries the annual total. `reportedCrimePeriods()` counts period presence, then `isUsableFbiAgencyYear()` separately rejects all-zero annual fake placeholders.
- The 2023 CDE response has 12 present periods but zero annual violent and property counts. It is intentionally rejected and must not be treated as low crime.
- 2020 national FBI reference rates are now encoded in `NATIONAL_CRIME_REFERENCE_BY_YEAR`, so this city can be calculated without custom one-off `--ref-*` values.
